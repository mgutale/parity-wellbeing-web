Cookie Policy

Clear information about how we use cookies.

Last updated: 27 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how ParityLabs Ltd ("we") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at www.paritylabs.uk and related domains.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies, categorised as follows:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They include:

  • Session management — to maintain your login state if you have an account
  • Security — to prevent fraudulent activity and protect the site

Analytics Cookies

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other third-party tracking scripts on our website. If we introduce privacy-respecting analytics in the future (e.g., Plausible or Posthog), we will list them here and obtain your consent before activating them.

Marketing Cookies

We do not use any marketing cookies. We do not track you across the web and we do not build advertising profiles.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, but please note that doing so may affect your ability to use certain features of our website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at privacy@paritylabs.uk.
