Our Story

From enterprise IT to personal wellbeing

Founded in London, ParityLabs began by delivering mission-critical software for regulated financial institutions. Over a decade, we honed our craft — building secure, scalable, and beautifully designed digital products.

With Parity Wellbeing, we're turning that expertise toward something deeply personal: mental health. We believe the same rigour applied to enterprise IT belongs in the wellbeing apps we trust with our most private moments.

2014

ParityLabs founded in London

Started as a boutique consultancy delivering bespoke development and IT operations to financial-sector clients.

2017

Expanding across regulated industries

Extended into healthcare and public-sector IT, deepening our understanding of data protection and compliance frameworks.

2021

Cloud-native and AI integration

Led enterprise migrations to AWS and Azure while building machine-learning pipelines for predictive analytics.

2023

Parity Wellbeing concept born

We saw a gap: no wellbeing app combined genuine clinical frameworks with truly private, beautiful UX. So we built it.

2026

Parity Wellbeing launches

Now available on the App Store, helping thousands track and improve their mental health with clinical-grade tools.
