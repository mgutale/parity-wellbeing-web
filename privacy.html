Privacy & Data Protection

How we collect, use, store and protect your personal data — and your rights under UK GDPR.

Privacy Policy GDPR Compliance
Last updated: 27 May 2026

ParityLabs Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Parity Wellbeing mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account data: Email address, encrypted password, and optional display name.
  • Wellbeing responses: Self-reported daily scores for Life Satisfaction, Happiness, Anxiety, and Worthwhileness using the UK ONS Personal Wellbeing framework. These are subjective 1–10 scale ratings, not medical diagnoses.
  • Journal data: Optional free-text entries and optional photo attachments.
  • Diagnostic data: Anonymous crash logs generated by the iOS operating system for bug fixing.

1.2 Sensitive Health Data Notice

Your wellbeing scores are self-reported subjective ratings, not medical data. We treat this information with the same safeguards as health data:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • We do not use wellbeing data for advertising, marketing, or third-party analytics
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your wellbeing data with any third party
  • We do not link your wellbeing data to third-party advertising identifiers
  • All AI insights are generated from your own data only via secure Supabase Edge Functions

1.3 Aggregate Data

We collect fully anonymised and aggregated demographic data (age range, gender, geographic region) solely to benchmark wellbeing trends against national ONS statistics. This data cannot identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the Parity Wellbeing service
  • To generate personalised insights and wellbeing reports for you
  • To send you optional reminders and motivational messages
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted within the European Union (Frankfurt region). We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • AES-256 encryption at rest
  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
  • Row-level security ensuring your data is isolated from other users
  • Regular automated security audits and penetration testing

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers (e.g., Supabase for database hosting, Apple for App Store processing) under strict data-processing agreements
  • Legal authorities only when required by UK law or a valid court order

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently erased within 90 days. Anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained for research purposes indefinitely.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@paritylabs.uk.

7. Children's Privacy

Parity Wellbeing is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses only essential cookies necessary for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

9. Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) within 72 hours.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notification and email at least 30 days before taking effect.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

  • Email: privacy@paritylabs.uk
  • Post: ParityLabs Ltd, London, United Kingdom

ParityLabs Ltd is the Data Controller for any personal data you provide through Parity Wellbeing. We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and take our data protection obligations seriously.

1. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: To provide the Parity Wellbeing service to you
  • Consent: For optional features such as push notifications and AI insights
  • Legitimate interests: For app improvement and security monitoring
  • Legal obligation: Where required by law or regulatory authority

2. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a UK data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Right to be informed — this page and our Privacy Policy fulfil this
  • Right of access — request a complete copy of your data
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate information
  • Right to erasure — delete your account and all associated data
  • Right to restrict processing — limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — export in JSON or CSV format
  • Right to object — opt out of analytics processing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making — any AI-generated insight can be reviewed by a human on request

3. Data We Process

Category Purpose Retention
Account credentials Authentication Duration of account
Wellbeing responses Score calculation & insights Duration of account
Journal entries Personal record & reflection Duration of account
Device / crash logs Bug fixing & stability 90 days
Aggregated demographics National benchmarking Indefinite (anonymised)

4. International Transfers

Your personal data is stored exclusively within the European Economic Area (EEA). We do not transfer personal data outside the EEA. If this changes in the future, we will only do so using adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

5. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

While not legally required for our size, we have appointed a dedicated Data Protection Lead:

  • Email: dpo@paritylabs.uk
  • Address: ParityLabs Ltd, London, United Kingdom

We aim to respond to all data subject requests within 14 days.

6. Data Breach Protocol

In the event of a breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the ICO within 72 hours
  • Notify affected users without undue delay
  • Provide clear steps to mitigate any risk
  • Conduct a full post-incident review and publish a lessons-learned report

7. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
