Our Story

From enterprise IT to personal wellbeing

Founded in London, ParityLabs began by delivering mission-critical software and infrastructure for regulated financial institutions in the UK and the Gulf. Over a decade, we honed our craft — building secure, scalable, and beautifully designed digital products.

With Parity Wellbeing, we're turning that expertise toward something deeply personal: mental health. We believe the same rigour applied to enterprise IT — security, reliability, and obsessive attention to user experience — belongs in the wellbeing apps we trust with our most private moments.

2014

ParityLabs founded in London

Started as a boutique consultancy delivering bespoke development and IT operations to financial-sector clients.

2017

Expanding across regulated industries

Extended into healthcare and public-sector IT, deepening our understanding of data protection and compliance frameworks.

2021

Cloud-native and AI integration

Led enterprise migrations to AWS and Azure while building machine-learning pipelines for predictive analytics.

2023

Parity Wellbeing concept born

We saw a gap: no wellbeing app combined genuine clinical frameworks with truly private, beautiful UX. So we built it.

2026

Parity Wellbeing launches

Now available on the App Store, helping thousands track and improve their mental health with clinical-grade tools.

Our Values

The principles that guide us

Every line of code we write and every design decision we make is filtered through these beliefs.

🛡️

Privacy by Design

Security isn't bolted on — it's the foundation. We encrypt, anonymise, and minimise data by default.

🔬

Clinical Rigor

We partner with health researchers and follow evidence-based frameworks — never shortcuts, never healthwashing.
