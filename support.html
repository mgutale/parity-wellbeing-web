Support Centre

Quick answers and how to get help with Parity Wellbeing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Parity Wellbeing a medical device?

No. Parity Wellbeing is a self-monitoring and wellness tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact your GP or call NHS 111 (UK) / your local emergency services.

Is my data really private?

Yes. Your wellbeing data is encrypted both at rest and in transit. We do not sell your data, use third-party ad trackers, or share identifiable information with anyone. See our Privacy Policy and GDPR page for full details.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your wellbeing history as a JSON or CSV file at any time from the Settings screen. This is useful if you want to share trends with a healthcare provider or simply keep your own offline backup.

How does the scoring work?

Parity Wellbeing uses the ONS Personal Wellbeing Survey framework — the same questions the UK government uses to measure national wellbeing. Each response is scored 0–10 across four dimensions: life satisfaction, feeling things are worthwhile, happiness, and anxiety. We also benchmark your scores anonymously against national averages.

What happens if I delete my account?

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your personal data from our servers within 90 days. Your anonymised demographic data (age range, region) may be retained for national benchmarking purposes, but it cannot be linked back to you.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can record check-ins and journal entries offline. The app syncs your data to secure cloud storage when you're back online. Some features (AI insights, benchmarking) require an internet connection.

Is there an Android version?

Parity Wellbeing is currently available for iOS. An Android version is planned for late 2026.

Will there be a subscription fee?

Parity Wellbeing is free to download and use. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but core wellbeing tracking will always remain free.
